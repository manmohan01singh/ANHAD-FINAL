# Sadhsangat Live - YouTube-Style Player Page ✅

## Problem Addressed
Previously, when users clicked a video in Sadhsangat Live, the app presented a temporary dark overlay popup centered on the screen with a simple close button, rather than a full, dedicated YouTube-style watch page interface.

## Solution & Features Implemented

### 1. 🎬 Dedicated YouTube Watch Page View (`.player-sheet`)
- **Full Watch View**: Clicking any video or live stream opens a dedicated watch view styled after YouTube.
- **Top Navigation Bar**: Features a clean `← Back` button, drag handle, and `Sadhsangat Watch` header.
- **Dynamic Ambient Lighting**: Soft ambient color glow surrounding the video container extracted dynamically from the video thumbnail.

### 2. 📺 16:9 High-Performance Video Viewport
- **Responsive Embed**: 16:9 aspect ratio video player supporting live streams and regular videos.
- **Interactive Gestures**: Double-tap left/right (10s skip backward/forward) with ripple HUD overlays.
- **Buffering & Audio Overlays**: Smooth buffering spinner and animated background equalizer overlay for audio-only mode.

### 3. 🏷️ Metadata & Channel Subscription Row
- **Video Title & Views Counter**: Crisp typography showing live streaming status or view counts.
- **Channel Info**: Channel avatar, channel name with gold checkmark, and a YouTube-style `Subscribe / Subscribed` toggle pill button.

### 4. 🎛️ Scrubber Timeline & Playback Action Pills
- **Timeline Scrubber**: Interactive timeline slider with elapsed time and total duration display.
- **Live Badge & Jump**: Real-time LIVE indicator badge with a 1-tap `Jump to Live` shortcut.
- **Action Pills Bar**: Like (with active highlight), Dislike, Share, Watch on YouTube, Gurbani GPT Vichar, and Playback Options.

### 5. ⏭️ YouTube-Style "Up Next" Feed
- **Recommended Feed**: Displays up to 12 recommended live streams and Gurbani videos directly under the player.
- **1-Tap Seamless Switching**: Tapping any video card in "Up Next" immediately loads and plays it in the player without closing the watch page.

### 6. 📱 Minimizable Floating Mini-Player
- **Smooth Minimize**: Tapping `Back` or dragging down on the top handle minimizes the player into a floating bottom mini-player bar.
- **Persistent Playback**: Users can continue browsing Sadhsangat channels while playback continues in the mini-player.
- **1-Tap Expand**: Tapping the mini-player bar expands it back to the full YouTube Watch Page.
