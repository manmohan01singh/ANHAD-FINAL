THE GENESIS STORY

How ANHAD Began

ੴ

Manmohan Singh

Sevadar

Electronics & Communication Engineering Student

📍 Punjab, India

The inspiration behind ANHAD was born out of a daily struggle. Every morning and evening, I found myself juggling between five to seven different apps:

📖 Nitnem Reader
📻 Gurbani Radio
🕌 Darbar Sahib Live
📿 Naam Simran
⏰ Amritvela Alarms
📝 Spiritual Notes

Each app had something good, but none provided a complete, peaceful, and uncluttered home for daily practice. I constantly asked myself:

"What if there was one peaceful sanctuary where any Gursikh around the world could find everything needed to stay connected with Gurbani throughout the day?"

That single heartfelt aspiration became ANHAD.

SEVA & ENGINEERING

Development Milestones

MONTH 1 • THE FOUNDATION

Authentic Gurmukhi Engine

Building a clean, zero-distraction Nitnem reader with accurate transliterations, translations, and typography.

MONTH 2-3 • AUDIO ARCHITECTURE

24×7 Virtual Live Broadcasting

Engineering global UTC-synchronized broadcast rotations for Amritvela Kirtan & Waheguru Simran, alongside direct live feeds from Sri Harmandir Sahib Ji.

MONTH 4-5 • INTERFACE REFINEMENT

Calm, Distraction-Free Design

Crafting a minimal 120 FPS interface, soft dynamic day & night palettes, and an ultra-deep Obsidian Onyx dark mode.

TODAY • THE UNIFIED COMPANION
