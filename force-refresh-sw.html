🔄 Force Service Worker Update

This will clear all caches and force reload of all updated files.

Step 1: Clear all Service Worker caches
Step 2: Unregister Service Workers
Step 3: Do ALL (Recommended)

Manual Steps (if buttons don't work):

  1. Press F12 to open DevTools
  2. Go to Application tab
  3. Click Service Workers
  4. Click Unregister on all workers
  5. Click Storage → Clear site data
  6. Close DevTools
  7. Press Ctrl+Shift+R to hard refresh
