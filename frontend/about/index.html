The Story

ANHAD began as a personal engineering project — a vision to bring together the best features of many Gurbani applications into one peaceful, unified experience.

Over approximately six months, this app was crafted with careful attention to detail, combining live Gurbani streams, Nitnem tracking, Naam Abhyas, Hukamnama, Sehaj Paath progress, and much more into a seamless interface.

The name "ANHAD" (ਅਨਹਦ) means "unstruck sound" — the eternal divine melody that resonates within. It represents the continuous flow of Gurbani Kirtan that connects us to Waheguru.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To create a sacred digital space where Sikhs worldwide can connect with Gurbani anytime, anywhere — fostering daily spiritual practice through technology that respects tradition.

Mission

To provide a comprehensive, peaceful, and intuitive Gurbani companion that supports:

  • 24/7 access to live Gurbani Kirtan and Darbar Sahib streams
  • Daily Nitnem tracking and habit building
  • Naam Abhyas and meditation support
  • Sehaj Paath progress tracking
  • Gurbani search, bookmarks, and personalization
  • Background playback for uninterrupted spiritual practice

Technology

ANHAD is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) wrapped with Capacitor for native Android functionality:

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla)
  • Backend: Node.js, Express
  • Native: Capacitor (Android wrapper)
  • APIs: BaniDB, YouTube Live, R2 Storage
  • Deployment: Vercel (web), Google Play (Android)
