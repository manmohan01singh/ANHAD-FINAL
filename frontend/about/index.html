Guru Sahib's Kirpa

ANHAD exists purely through the infinite grace and blessings of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji. "Anhad" (ਅਨਹਦ) represents the unstruck celestial melody — the continuous divine vibration connecting the soul with Waheguru.

ਅਨਹਦ ਸਬਦ ਦਸਮ ਦੁਆਰਿ ਵਜਿਓ ਤਹ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਨਾਮੁ ਚੁਆਇਆ ॥

"The unstruck sound current resounds in the Tenth Gate, where the Ambrosial Nectar of the Naam trickles down." (Ang 1002)
