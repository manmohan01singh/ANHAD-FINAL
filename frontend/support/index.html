📖 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable Nitnem reminders?

Go to Settings → Notifications and enable "Daily Nitnem Reminders". You can customize the time for each Bani.

Why isn't the live stream playing?

Check your internet connection. If the issue persists, the stream source may be temporarily unavailable. Try switching to another stream (Virtual Live, Amritvela).

How do I change the theme?

Navigate to Settings → Appearance → Theme and choose Light, Dark, or Auto (changes based on time of day).

Can I use ANHAD offline?

Most Banis in the Nitnem section work offline after the first load. However, live streaming and Hukamnama require an internet connection.

How do I track Sehaj Paath progress?

Open the Sehaj Paath section and mark Angs as you complete them. Your progress is saved locally on your device.

What if I lost my Nitnem streak data?

Data is stored locally. If you clear app data or uninstall the app, your streak will be reset. We recommend regular backups if this feature is important to you.

How do I bookmark a Shabad?

While reading a Shabad, tap the bookmark icon. Access your bookmarks anytime from the Favorites page.

Why did my bookmarks disappear?

Bookmarks are stored locally. Clearing browser data, cache, or uninstalling the app will delete them. Always export your data if available.

🐛 Known Issues

  • Audio stuttering on some devices: Try reducing background apps or restarting the app
  • Theme not applying immediately: Close and reopen the app
  • Notification delays: Check battery optimization settings and allow ANHAD to run in the background

We're actively working on fixes. Check the Changelog for updates.

💬 Report a Bug

Found a bug? Help us improve ANHAD by reporting it:

  1. Go to Contact page
  2. Describe the issue in detail (steps to reproduce, screenshots if possible)
  3. Include your device model and Android version

We appreciate your patience as we work to resolve issues.
