THE GENESIS STORY

How ANHAD Began

ੴ

Manmohan Singh

Sevadar

Electronics & Communication Engineering Student

📍 Punjab, India

The inspiration behind ANHAD came from a daily struggle. Every morning and evening, I found myself switching between five to seven different applications:

📖 Nitnem Reader
📻 Gurbani Radio
🕌 Darbar Sahib Live
📿 Naam Simran
⏰ Amritvela Alarms
📝 Spiritual Notes

Each app had something good, but none brought everything together into one serene, distraction-free environment. I kept thinking:

"What if there was one peaceful place where any Gursikh around the world could find everything needed to stay connected with Gurbani throughout the day?"

That single heartfelt aspiration became ANHAD.

DEVELOPMENT CHRONICLES

Milestones & Journey

MONTH 1 • THE FOUNDATION

Authentic Gurmukhi Engine

Building a distraction-free Nitnem reader with accurate transliterations, translations, and custom fonts.

MONTH 2-3 • AUDIO SUBSYSTEM

24×7 Virtual Live Broadcasting

Engineering UTC-synchronized television-style broadcast rotations for Kirtan & Simran alongside direct Icecast feeds from Sri Harmandir Sahib Ji.

MONTH 4-5 • INTERFACE REFINEMENT

120 FPS Fluid Design System

Crafting Apple-grade claymorphism, dynamic day/night ambient palettes, and an ultra-deep Obsidian Onyx dark mode.

TODAY • THE COMPANION

Complete Offline Sanctuary

Cross-platform PWA, native Android & iOS engines, unified streak analytics, and continuous community improvements.
