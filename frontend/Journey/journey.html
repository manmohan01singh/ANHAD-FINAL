🌿 How ANHAD Began

Manmohan Singh

Electronics & Communication Engineering Student

📍 Punjab, India

The idea behind ANHAD came from a simple thought.

Every day I found myself opening different apps for different things.

One app for Nitnem.
Another for Gurbani Radio.
Another for live Darbar Sahib.
Another for Naam Simran.
Another for reminders.
Another for bookmarks.

Every app had something good, but none of them brought everything together.

I kept thinking,

"What if there was one peaceful place where a Sikh could find everything needed to stay connected with Gurbani throughout the day?"

That single thought became ANHAD.

💛 This Journey

Over the previous six months, alongside my engineering studies, ANHAD slowly grew feature by feature.

There were days when progress felt easy, and there were days when a single bug took hours—or even days—to understand.

Many features were redesigned several times before they finally felt right.

Each challenge became another lesson, not only in programming but also in patience.

Throughout this journey, I never saw ANHAD as just another software project.

For me, it became a way to learn, to improve, and to contribute in whatever small way I could.

💡 What ANHAD Offers Today

Today, ANHAD brings together many features in one peaceful place:

🎵 Darbar Sahib Live
🌅 Amritvela Kirtan
📿 Waheguru Simran

And by Guru Sahib's kirpa, there is still much more planned.
