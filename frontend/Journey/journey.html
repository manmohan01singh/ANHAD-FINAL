THE GENESIS STORY

How ANHAD Began

ੴ

Manmohan Singh

Sevadar

Electronics & Communication Engineering Student

📍 Punjab, India

The inspiration for ANHAD came from a daily struggle. Every morning and evening, I found myself switching between five to seven different apps:

📖 Nitnem Reader
📻 Gurbani Radio
🕌 Darbar Sahib Live
📿 Naam Simran
⏰ Amritvela Alarms
📝 Spiritual Notes

Each app had something good, but none provided a complete, peaceful, and unified sanctuary for everyday practice. I constantly asked myself:

“

What if there was one serene, distraction-free home where any Gursikh around the world could find everything needed to stay connected with Gurbani, all day long?

That single heartfelt thought became ANHAD.

Development Milestones

MONTH 1 • THE FOUNDATION

The First Sacred Step

Designing an authentic Gurmukhi typography engine and a zero-distraction Nitnem reader with accurate transliterations and translations.

24×7 Virtual Live Broadcast

Engineering a global UTC-synchronized broadcast rotation for Amritvela Kirtan & Waheguru Simran, paired with direct high-fidelity Icecast feeds from Sri Harmandir Sahib Ji.

Next-Gen UI/UX & 120 FPS Rendering

Crafting supreme claymorphism, dynamic time-of-day sky backdrops (Morning, Day, Evening, Night), and a deep Obsidian Onyx dark mode.
