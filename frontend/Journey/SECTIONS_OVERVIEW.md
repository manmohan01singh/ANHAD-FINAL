## 🎨 Design Features

### Colors
- **Primary**: Saffron/Gold gradient (#d4a574 → #f0d890)
- **Accent**: Mint/Green for secondary elements
- **Background**: Auto light/dark theme

### Animations
- ✨ Fade-in on scroll
- 🎈 Floating logo
- 📍 Timeline dots pulse on hover
- 🎯 Cards lift on hover
- ↗️ Arrow slides on hover
- 🌊 Parallax background in cover

### Interactive Elements
- All cards have hover effects
- Back button (ESC key also works)
- Smooth scrolling
- Version loads automatically
- Theme switches automatically

---

## 📱 Responsive Breakpoints

- **Desktop** (900px+): Full width, 3 columns for stats
- **Tablet** (768px - 900px): 2 columns
- **Mobile** (< 768px): Single column, adjusted spacing

---

## 🎯 Purpose of Each Section

1. **Cover** - Eye-catching intro, sets the tone
2. **Welcome** - Thanks users, explains purpose
3. **Creator** - Shows it's built by a real person
4. **Journey** - Story of development (not a brag)
5. **Timeline** - Shows the evolution process
6. **Statistics** - What's included (facts, not hype)
7. **Why Free** - Spirit of seva, not business
8. **Thanks** - Gratitude to everyone
9. **Contact** - Open door for feedback
10. **Footer** - Version & blessing

---

## 💡 Key Philosophy

**This page tells the story of ANHAD, not the story of you.**

The focus remains on:
- Why it exists (seva)
- How it evolved (journey)
- What it offers (features)
- Who to thank (gratitude)

You appear in the story, but you're not the main character.
**The Sangat and Guru Sahib are the main characters.**
