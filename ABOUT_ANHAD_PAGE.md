## 🎨 Design Details

### Colors:
- **Gold gradient**: #d4a574 → #f0d890 (titles, accents)
- **Backgrounds**: Soft white (light) / Dark (dark mode)
- **Cards**: Glass morphism with backdrop blur

### Typography:
- **System fonts**: SF Pro Display, Segoe UI
- **Sizes**: 2.5rem (hero) → 1.05rem (body)
- **Weights**: 700 (bold) → 400 (normal)

### Spacing:
- **Sections**: 16px gap between cards
- **Padding**: 32px inside cards
- **Safe areas**: iOS notch support

### Animations:
- Logo floating (3s ease-in-out)
- Cards hover lift
- Fade-in on load
